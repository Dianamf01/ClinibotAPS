# ClinibotAPS
CliniBot APS is a conversational agent built in Microsoft Copilot Studio to support the first step of medical consultations in Mexico’s primary care clinics. It welcomes the patient, adapts to how they feel, collects relevant health information, and creates a clear summary to help the doctor. 

This project is designed by me Diana a student, passionate about creating meaningful solutions using accessible technology.

##  What CliniBot APS Does

- Greets the patient with empathy and requests **informed consent**.
- Identifies the **type of patient** (adult, child, older adult, pregnant person).
- Adapts its **style of questioning** depending on the patients emotional state (calm, nervous, frustrated).
- Collects essential information: current symptoms, medications, lifestyle, and social support.
- Generates a short **clinical summary** for the treating physician.
- Always respects the patient's decision and privacy.

##  Why it Matters

In many clinics in Mexico, patients line up from 5:00 a.m. and wait for hours. Physicians work under time pressure. CliniBot APS helps optimize this process by:
- Offering a **personalized first interaction**
- **Saving time** for doctors
- **Giving voice** to the patient before the consultation

This bot is not intended to replace doctors or give diagnoses. It collaborates with them.
